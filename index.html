CPA in this workbook

The Brand Visibility total is calculated from contribution and new users.

CPA = total CM1 ÷ total new users

A negative result means a CM1 shortfall per new user. This is not a conventional paid-media CPA because Sheet5 has no separate marketing-spend field.

GMV

Gross merchandise value is the reported booking value recorded for each redemption and summed by partner and period.

Reported GMV ≈ total fare + convenience fee

15,617 of 15,619 Sheet5 rows reconcile within ₹1; two January 2025 rows have incomplete numeric fare / GMV fields. GMV measures booking value, not ixigo revenue or profit.

CM1

First-level contribution after the offer discount and payment gateway charge.

CM1 = GR − instant discount − PG charge

CM1 margin = total CM1 ÷ total GMV. The formula matches all 15,619 redemption rows in Sheet5 within source rounding.
